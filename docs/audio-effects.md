# Original audio effects, voices and item icons

The port keeps the PC Engine soundtrack as CD-DA and adds the short effects
through the Mega CD RF5C164 PCM chip.  No effect is generated by the port's
sound code: every byte comes from a capture of the commercial game.

## Packed PCM effects

`game/res/audio/city_sfx.bin` is produced by `tools/pack_city_sfx.py`:

| Bank | Effect | Source window | Length |
|---:|---|---|---:|
| 0 | sword | PCE city PSG residual, 11.00–11.40 s | 3,308 bytes |
| 1 | jump | Genesis Plus GX PSG-only B-button difference | 3,308 bytes |
| 2 | hit | PCE city PSG residual, 15.95–16.40 s | 2,646 bytes |
| 3 | magic | PCE city PSG residual, 19.95–20.40 s | 2,536 bytes |

The PCE residual is the original city recording (`build/city-audio/pce.wav`)
minus the known CD-DA track 38.  The measured alignment is 52.8577 s with a
0.49 gain; the residual therefore contains the PSG effects that the retail mix
placed under the music.  The Genesis jump window comes from the user's
Japanese cartridge through Genesis Plus GX, with the FM channels and YM2612 DAC
muted, as the difference against a neutral run from the same save state.

The packer emits 8-bit sign-magnitude data (0x80 = +0, 0x01..0x7f negative,
0x80..0xfe positive, 0xff loop marker), 256-byte aligned.  Each sample ends in
`0x80 0xff`; the loop registers point at the silent `0x80` byte.  The four
samples occupy three 4 KiB PCM banks.

The Sub CPU uploads those banks once (`prepare_city_sfx`), then
`CMD_PLAY_SFX` starts channel 2 with the 11,025 Hz pitch (register value 694).
The player voice starts at bank 3 and remains on channel 1, so dialogue and
effects can overlap.  The Main CPU requests an effect from the same command
gateway for jumps, sword swings, damage, magic and item pickup.

`tools/test_city_sfx.py` selects the boss scene, jumps and attacks, serializes
the emulator state and checks that the exact jump and sword sample bytes are
present in Mega CD PCM RAM.  `tools/test_city_voice.py` does the same for
Cham's voice bank during the meeting, and `tools/test_city_cdda.py` confirms
that the city music and the voiced line still play in the mixed output.  The
sample sources and test outputs are local build artifacts; the packed binary
is checked in.

## Item icons

`tools/pack_pce_icons.py` extracts four motifs from the original PCE city HUD
capture (`build/city-audio/pce-06300.bmp`): the sword gem, the character
portrait, the ring badge and the bell badge.  Their colours are mapped to the
captured city palette line 1, which the rooftop background does not use.  The
icons are 2x2 hardware sprites at tiles 1280–1295; the captured CRAM is left
unchanged, so `tools/test_city_integration.py` still verifies all six hardware
palettes against the Genesis capture.

## Own voices and the dungeon boss

The city keeps the PCE prologue and the voiced boss conversation on CD-DA
tracks 22/23 and the rescue on track 24 as before.  STAGE2C now hands off to
`STG2BOSS.MMD`, which plays the PCE encounter conversation track 23 and then
repeats the PCE boss track 27 while the fight runs.  The same scene is also
selectable from the title.  The boss pair, its fireballs and the corridor are
extracted from the STG2C save-state capture; the player uses the captured
STG2A frames.  Boss timing, 16 hit points, projectile pattern and collision
are port gameplay.
